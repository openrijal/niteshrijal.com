The world of Personal AI Agents (bots) have grown, and all major players now have a version. [GrokBot](https://x.ai/bot) still gets most of my work. [Spark](https://gemini.google/overview/agent/spark/) has a clear job inside my Google account. [Muse](https://muse.ai/) is there, but I don’t reach for it as much. And now I’ve added a [Dot](https://chatgpt.com/features/dots/).

In my [earlier post](https://niteshrijal.com/blog/handing-both-of-them-the-same-work/), I wrote about handing Muse and GrokBot the same work.

## Spark handles my Google stuff

Spark is what I call Gemini. I didn’t have to connect Gmail, Calendar and Drive separately. My Google stuff was already there.

I ask questions about it, or give Spark something to do in my Google account. That’s basically its job for me. It’s my Google manager. It has other capabilities, and can do other stuff, but its interface is not as great, and I feel its very limited. I just give it light work.

It has a computer too, but that part is limited, and I can’t take control of it. The Google side is where it fits into my day.

## GrokBot gets the most work

I have GrokBot on my phone, MacBook and Linux box. It can access my computers, and I can access its computer. It can log into sites and do work I’d otherwise have to do myself.

[Balakatha](https://balakatha.app) is a good example. I can have it generate videos on my linux box and upload them to Meta, TikTok and YouTube. The video starts on one place and ends up on several others. I can give it that work instead of walking each site myself.

I also create specialized bots with scoped work. Different apps I develop have their own bots, with the relevant Cloudflare and GitHub access ready for them.

For coding, it can talk to [Cursor](https://cursor.com/). The coding work can go through GrokBot too.

## Muse is useful but gets less use

I’ve tried Muse with Gmail and Meta. It can do useful work, and its computer is good. In my use, GrokBot’s computer has been better.

Muse is more like one bot with topic-specific side chats. With GrokBot, I’ve set up multiple specialized bots. That difference matters when I’m working across different apps.

I can see uses for Muse, like Facebook Marketplace. That’s still a possible use for me. I don’t use Muse as much.

## Dot is the new one

Dot can retrieve my past ChatGPT conversations. It also has its own cloud browser and workspace. Coding work can go into separate [Codex](https://chatgpt.com/codex/) tasks in a supported environment.

I haven’t run my usual website or coding jobs through it yet, and my Google accounts aren’t connected to it. There’s still a lot I can’t compare from experience. The similarity here is to Muse more because it has just 1 bot or dot.

I’m starting with this post. I gave my Dot an earlier article and asked for an outline, a capability comparison and a draft in my language. That’s its first writing test with me. I edited a bit inline in the ChatGPT app with codex window sidebar.

## The comparison so far

The table is about my use. “Available” is still untested for that job. Where I haven’t tested or compared something, I’ve left the judgment open.

| What I need | Gemini / Spark | GrokBot | Muse | Dot |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Google work | Gmail, Calendar and Drive in use | Gmail connected | Gmail connected | Available; not connected |
| Website tasks | Limited computer | Used for logins and tasks | Tried; less used | Cloud browser available |
| Taking over its computer | I can’t take control | I can access it | Not compared here | Not tested by me |
| Work on my computers | Not tested by me | MacBook and Linux access | Not compared here | Mac connected; first task underway |
| Separating work | Google manager | Specialized bots per app | One bot with side chats | Separate Codex tasks available |
| Coding | Not tested by me | Works with Cursor | Not compared here | Codex available |
| Video and social work | Not tested by me | Balakatha to Meta, TikTok and YouTube | Meta connected; limited use | Not tested by me |
| Role in my setup | Google manager | Main assistant | Occasional use | Just starting |

## My verdict for now

GrokBot stays my main assistant. It already does the website work and has specialized bots around my apps. Spark handles my Google account. Muse has possible uses, but I haven’t been reaching for it as much.

Dot is too new for a verdict. I’m giving it something concrete to do before deciding where it belongs.
